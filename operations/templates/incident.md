# Incident Template

Use this for customer-impacting or service-impacting events. Severity is based on business impact, not technical complexity.

## Required fields

- Incident ID: `INC-####`
- Title
- Severity: `P1 | P2 | P3 | P4`
- Status: `New | Acknowledged | Investigating | Mitigating | Monitoring | Escalated | Resolved | Closed`
- Owner
- Source
- Service / affected feature
- Detected timestamp
- Acknowledged timestamp once acknowledged
- Customer impact
- Known scope
- Timeline
- Evidence
- Current mitigation / resolution
- Recovery validation
- Linked problem/change/runbook records when applicable

## Minimum timeline pattern

`detect -> acknowledge -> impact/scope -> evidence -> hypothesis -> mitigation/escalation -> recovery validation -> follow-up`
