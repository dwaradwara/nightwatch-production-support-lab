# Shift Handover Template

## Shift window

- outgoing owner/team
- incoming owner/team
- shift start/end

## Service health

Summarize current customer-facing health, degraded components, and unusual telemetry. Avoid "all good" without evidence.

## Open incidents

For each open incident include severity, status, owner, customer impact, latest evidence, last update, and next required action.

## Scheduled changes

List approved or pending changes, maintenance windows, expected impact, and rollback owner.

## Open problems / known errors

List recurring issues, active workarounds, and conditions that should trigger escalation.

## Risks

Call out capacity, certificate, storage, dependency, release, or operational risks likely to matter during the next shift.

## Next actions

Each action must have an owner or clear receiving role and a concrete trigger/deadline where applicable.
